How a project actually goes.

  1. 01

    The audit

    We look at your site the way one of your customers would, on a phone, and show you exactly where it loses them.

  2. 02

    The draft

    You get a written quote and a contract in plain English. Then we design, and you see real work before the balance is due.

  3. 03

    The build

    We build it by hand, you review it on your own phone, and we revise until it's right. Two rounds are included in every quote.

  4. 04

    The launch

    We handle the domain, hosting, and Google. After that, stay on a Care Plan or walk away with everything. Your call.

Ask a question
What happens if I don't like it?

Two rounds of revisions are included in every quote, and you see real design work before the second half is due. If you walk away after the first payment, you owe nothing further and we keep nothing of yours.

Who actually builds my site?

Mukil and Sarthak. The two of us, personally. Not an intern, not a contractor, not a template with your logo dropped in. The people who build it are the people who answer when you write.

How long does a project take?

A Refresh usually takes two to three weeks; a Rebuild, three to four. We agree on a delivery date in the contract before any money changes hands.

What do you need from me?

About an hour of your time, your photos and hours, and honest answers to a short intake form. We handle everything else, including writing the words.

Do I own the site when it's done?

Completely. Domain, files, accounts, everything is in your name the day the final payment clears. No hostage situations.

Why are you cheaper than agencies?

Because we're students with no office and no payroll, not because the work is worth less. Compare the before-and-afters above and decide for yourself.

What happens after launch?

Your choice. Join the Care Plan and there's a real person on call for updates and fixes, or take the keys and go. Either way, nothing breaks when you leave.

What if I don't have a website at all?

That's the Rebuild, and it's often easier than fixing an old site. We'll still send you a free audit first, looking at your Google listing and what people find when they search for you.

What if I need more than 5 pages?

We'll quote it. Five pages covers most local businesses, and anything beyond that is priced before we start, in writing, so the number never moves after you've said yes.

How do I pay you?

Half to start, half when the site goes live, with a written invoice for each. We'll agree on the method that's easiest for you before anything is due, and we never ask for card details.
